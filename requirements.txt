
---

## 🛠 Requirements
- Python 3.10+
- `requests`
- `tkinter` (included with Python)
- `zipfile` (included with Python)

---

## 🧪 Integrity Checker
The **Verify existing files** button will:

- Scan all `.osz` files  
- Remove missing files from the manifest  
- Detect corrupted files  
- Automatically re-download them  
- Update the manifest  

---

## 🔧 Configuration

### Thread count  
Adjustable slider (1–16 threads).

### Mirror priority  
Drag mirrors up/down in the settings window.

### Auto-import  
Automatically opens `.osz` files after download.
